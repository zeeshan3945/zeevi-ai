About us

The team behind your training data

ZeeVi.ai is a computer-vision data and AI studio. We help agriculture, industrial and robotics teams turn raw imagery into production-ready datasets and models — covering image segmentation, object detection and full AI/CV solution delivery.

We've delivered 170+ projects and large-scale annotation programs for clients including The Land Institute and FutureCalc.ai. We're currently running a multi-year video-annotation program — 300,000 hours of footage with a workforce of 100+ annotators scaling toward 400 — with a relentless focus on annotation quality, consistency and turnaround.
